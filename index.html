Popular Destinations

Swat Valley

Green meadows, rivers and the famous Malam Jabba ski resort.

Hunza Valley

Apricot orchards, ancient forts and views of Rakaposhi peak.

Fairy Meadows

A high-altitude meadow facing Nanga Parbat, reached by a scenic trek.

Skardu

Gateway to K2, with Shangrila Lake and the cold desert at Katpana.

Naran Kaghan

Lake Saif-ul-Malook and alpine scenery in the Kaghan Valley.

Chitral

Home of the Kalash valleys and the towering Tirich Mir.

About Us

Northern Trails is a student project travel agency concept based in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. We plan safe, affordable trips and focus on responsible tourism that supports local communities.

Travel Tips

  • Best season: May to September
  • Carry warm clothes, even in summer
  • Check road conditions before you leave
  • Keep a copy of your CNIC or passport
